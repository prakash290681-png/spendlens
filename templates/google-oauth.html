How SpendLens Uses Google Data

What Data Is Accessed

SpendLens requests read-only access to Gmail to detect order confirmation emails from food delivery platforms.

How Data Is Used

Email content is analyzed to extract transaction information such as:

  • Merchant name
  • Transaction amount
  • Date and time
This information is used to generate spending insights.

What Data Is Not Used

  • No access to contacts
  • No sending emails
  • No modification of emails
  • No selling or sharing of data

Data Deletion

Users can delete their data at any time by using the delete account option or contacting support.
