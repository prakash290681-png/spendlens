Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Overview

SpendLens helps users track food delivery spending by analyzing order-related emails from Gmail. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

2. Data We Access

After you sign in using Google OAuth, SpendLens reads only email content necessary to detect order confirmations from services such as Swiggy, Zomato, Instamart, Zepto and similar platforms.

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not store your Gmail password.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share personal information with third parties.

4. Data Storage

We store only extracted transaction information such as merchant name, amount and date, required to provide analytics and insights.

5. Security

Authentication is handled securely through Google OAuth. All data is transmitted using HTTPS encryption.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@spendlens.app
